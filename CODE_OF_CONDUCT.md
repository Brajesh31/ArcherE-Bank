# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Commitment

ArcherE-Bank welcomes constructive participation from people with varied backgrounds and experience levels. Project spaces should remain respectful, inclusive and focused on improving the codebase.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and critique code or ideas, not people.
- Explain concerns with evidence and actionable suggestions.
- Welcome good-faith questions.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Use inclusive language and accessible communication.
- Keep discussion professional and relevant.
- Protect credentials and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, doxxing and publishing private information are unacceptable. Never post credentials, identity records, card details, CVVs, session cookies or database exports.

## Reporting and enforcement

Applies to issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews and project spaces. Report concerns privately to the maintainer [@Brajesh31](https://github.com/Brajesh31). The maintainer may edit/remove content, reject contributions or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and prioritize safety.
